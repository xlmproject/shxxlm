02 / The network beneath

Built on Stellar.

A global settlement layer designed to move value with speed, efficiency and open access.

A network made for movement.

Stellar is an open-source, public blockchain built for payments and digital assets. It lets people and businesses issue, hold and exchange tokenized value, with a built-in decentralized exchange and low-cost transactions. Its Stellar Consensus Protocol helps the network reach agreement without energy-intensive mining.
